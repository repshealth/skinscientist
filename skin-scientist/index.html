Acids and chemical exfoliation

How acids work

Alpha hydroxy acids
  • Water soluble
  • Work on the surface, loosening the bonds between dead cells
  • Best for texture, dullness and pigmentation

Smallest AHA molecule, so it works fastest. Boosts collagen, best for normal to dry skin.

Beta hydroxy acid (salicylic)
  • Oil soluble
  • Travels into the follicle and breaks down the oil and debris inside
  • Best for congestion, blackheads and oily skin
Polyhydroxy acids
  • Large molecule, so it stays right on the surface
  • Hydrates while it gently exfoliates
  • Best for sensitive skin
